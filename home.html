About Our Platform

What is Re-Earth?

Re-Earth is a waste bank platform that empowers individuals and communities to take action against plastic pollution. By providing an easy-to-use system for sorting and depositing household waste, Re-Earth encourages sustainable habits and rewards users for their efforts. Our mission is to create a cleaner and greener environment.

Frequently Asked Questions

All you need to know about how our waste bank works, its benefits, and how you can participate.

Re-Earth Waste Bank is a platform that allows individuals to deposit their household waste and earn rewards. We provide collection, weighing, and waste management services.
To join, you can contact us through our "Contact Us" page. We will guide you through the registration process.
Before depositing, please separate your trash into categories such as plastics, paper, glass, and metals. Ensure they are clean, dry, and flattened.
You can drop off your sorted trash at the collection point nearest to you. We have multiple drop-off locations across the city.
The rates vary depending on the type and quality of the waste. You can view our updated daily waste price list directly on Our Price List.
